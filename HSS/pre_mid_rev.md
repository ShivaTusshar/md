# Sociology Exam — Quick Revision Notes

---

## 1. Mills – *The Sociological Imagination*, "The Promise"
- **Sociological Imagination**: linking **biography ↔ history ↔ social structure**
- **Personal troubles** (private, individual milieu) vs **Public issues** (structural, institutional) — e.g., 1 unemployed man = trouble; 15M unemployed = issue
- **3 questions** classic analysts ask: (1) structure of society (2) where it stands in history (3) what human types it produces
- Critiques: **Grand Theory** (too abstract) vs **Abstracted Empiricism** (too factual, no theory) — wants balance

## 2. Deshpande – "Squinting at Society"
- **Squinting** = seeing both commonsense (the pane) AND the world beyond it
- Sociology = **critique of commonsense**
- 3 roots of commonsense: **Phenomenology** (Husserl/Schutz—shared background understanding), **Gramsci** (ideology, consent, subversion possible), **Bourdieu's doxa** (unquestionable, precludes even disagreement)
- **3 peculiarities of Indian sociology**: (1) ambivalent colonial legacy (anthropology resented, history/economics celebrated) (2) sociology fused with anthropology → weak discipline (3) anxiety over "Indian-ness"

## 3. Halfpenny – "Comte and the Early Period"
- **Law of 3 Stages**: Theological → Metaphysical → Positive (science = observed invariable relations/laws)
- **Hierarchy of sciences**: astronomy → physics → chemistry → biology → **sociology** (queen of sciences); psychology excluded
- Comte = **order + progress** combined; anti-individualist; society = organic whole
- **Religion of Humanity** = later, discredited turn (Positivism₄)
- **Spencer**: evolution via individual competition (not Comte's harmony) = Positivism₅
- **Durkheim**: fused Comte + statistics (*Suicide*) = Positivism₆

## 4. Durkheim – "What is a Social Fact?"
- 2 criteria: **Externality** (pre-exists individual) + **Constraint** (coercive, visible when resisted)
- Social facts = *sui generis*, irreducible to individual/biological
- **Not defined by generality** — general BECAUSE collective, not vice versa
- **Rates** (suicide, marriage) prove facts exist independent of individuals
- **Morphological facts** (structural: law, architecture) vs **currents** (fluid: crowd enthusiasm) — differ in degree of crystallization, not kind
- Final def: way of acting, external + constraining, OR general + independent of individual manifestations
- Rejects **Tarde's imitation theory**

## 5. Weber – "Basic Sociological Terms"
- Sociology = **interpretive understanding (Verstehen)** + **causal explanation**
- Action = subjective meaning attached; **Social action** = oriented to others
- **Ideal type**: pure rational construct as methodological benchmark (not real)
- **Verstehen types**: direct observational vs explanatory (motive-based)
- Two tests: **adequate on level of meaning** + **causally adequate**
- **4 types of social action**: Zweckrational (instrumental), Wertrational (value), Affectual, Traditional
- Collectivities (state, family) = NOT real actors, only organized individual action
- **Social relationship** = probability of meaningfully oriented mutual action (can be asymmetrical)

## 6. Schutz – *Phenomenology of the Social World*
- Ch.2: Meaning arises only via **reflective attention** turned toward elapsed experience (not while living it)
- **In-order-to motive** (future, purpose) vs **Because-motive** (past, retrospective cause)
- Ch.3: **Alter Ego** taken for granted; **Fremdverstehen** (genuine understanding via bodily presence/simultaneity) vs self-elucidation (mere typing)
- **Umwelt** (consociates, direct) → **Mitwelt** (contemporaries, indirect/ideal types) → Vorwelt (predecessors) → Folgewelt (successors)
- **Thou-orientation** → We-relationship (face-to-face); **They-orientation** (anonymous, typified)
- Social science = objective meaning-context built from subjective ones

## 7. Marx & Engels – *Communist Manifesto*
- **History = history of class struggles**
- Modern era simplifies into 2 classes: **bourgeoisie vs proletariat**
- Bourgeoisie: revolutionary, dissolves feudal ties → "all that is solid melts into air"; creates world market/globalization
- **Crises of overproduction** = "too much civilisation" → bourgeoisie is sorcerer who can't control forces summoned
- Proletariat stages: individual resistance → machine-smashing → trade unions → political class
- Only proletariat is truly revolutionary (others are conservative, trying to save their status)
- Bourgeoisie produces "**its own grave-diggers**" — fall inevitable

## 8. Bourdieu – "Structures, Habitus, Practices"
- Against **Objectivism** (rules/structure only) and **Subjectivism** (pure free choice)
- **Habitus** = durable, transposable dispositions; "structured structures predisposed to function as structuring structures"
- Generates practice WITHOUT conscious calculation; "regulated improvisation," orchestrated without a conductor
- **Embodied history**, "forgotten as history" (quotes Durkheim on unconscious past selves)
- **Hysteresis effect**: habitus lags behind changed conditions (Don Quixote example)
- Class habitus = shared dispositions from shared conditions; individual habitus = variant of it
- Critiques rational-choice economics — "feel for the game" (**illusio** + **doxa**) replaces calculation

## 9. Bourdieu – "The Forms of Capital"
- Social world = accumulated history; NOT like **roulette** (no inertia/inheritance)
- **3 capitals**: Economic (property), Cultural (education), Social (connections/title of nobility)
- **Cultural capital 3 states**: Embodied (dispositions, needs time, can't be transferred instantly) / Objectified (books, art) / Institutionalized (qualifications — "collective magic," neutralizes person-dependency)
- Embodied capital = **misrecognized as natural talent** = symbolic capital
- **Social capital** = network + capital of those connected; needs "labour of sociability"; often **delegated** to a representative
- **Conversion principle**: all capital rooted in economic (labor-time) but effective BECAUSE it disguises this

## 10. Subramanian – "Making Merit" (IITs)
- IITs = seen as pure meritocracy (JEE exam, "Brand IIT") but built on **caste capital converted to modern capital**
- **Deshpande's "castelessness"**: upper castes = unmarked "general category"/merit norm; lower castes = hyper-visible, caste-marked
- **General category = merit-based** category (semantic slippage)
- Subramanian's addition: castelessness is NOT stable/complete — it's **relational**, shifts based on subaltern (lower-caste) assertion
- Tamil Nadu case: Dravidian movement made Brahmins hyper-visible → they claim merit THROUGH caste (not despite it)
- "Passing in reverse" (Nagaraj example) — OBC student assumed Brahmin at IIT
- Post-2006 OBC quota: "general category" became explicitly marked as **upper-caste**, not neutral
- Béteille (counter-view): caste irrelevant for urban professionals, quotas/politics perpetuate caste — Subramanian disputes this

## 11. Harriss-White – *India Working*, Ch. 2
- Only **~7%** organised sector; **83–93%** unorganised/informal — "India's economy IS unorganised"
- **Jobless growth**: 5% GDP growth but 0.1% organised employment growth (1977–94)
- Workforce: 56% self-employed, 29% casual, 15% regular/salaried
- **Debt-bondage/patronage** ties labour; Breman's "**neo-bondage**" = monetized but loses old protective element
- Labour markets **highly fragmented/localized** → blocks collective action
- **Gender**: women work more, paid less (~71% of male wage), unpaid domestic labour subsidizes system
- **Child labour**: ~40 million — not that economy needs it, but families need children's income
- **Caste**: stratifies pay, creates "non-competing" segmented markets; SCs = casual/poor/menial; reservations paradox = entrenches caste as interest group
- Caste used BOTH to fragment labour (by employers) AND to organize labour (by workers) — not "false consciousness" (Gooptu)
- Weak unions (party-captured, fragmented), toothless laws, state complicit with capital
- Key line: **"struggle over class" (labour) vs "struggle between classes" (capital)**

## Golden Thread Across All Readings
- **Mills / Weber / Schutz** → meaning & interpretation (micro, subjective)
- **Durkheim / Marx** → structure & constraint (macro, objective)
- **Bourdieu** → bridges both (habitus / capital)
- **Deshpande / Subramanian / Harriss-White** → apply theory to **Indian caste-class realities**
